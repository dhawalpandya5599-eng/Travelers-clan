# Lesson 17: India destinations, deep dive

Goa peak season is November to February.
Goa shoulder season is March and October.
North Goa is for nightlife and water sports.
South Goa is for quiet beaches and families.
Palolem beach is the calm choice in South Goa.
Baga and Calangute are the crowded beaches in North Goa.
Dudhsagar falls is best from October to January.
Goa December hotel rates are the highest of the year.
Manali season is March to June and September to December.
Manali snow is reliable from late December to February.
Solang valley offers paragliding and zorbing.
Atal tunnel is the day trip route to Sissu and Lahaul.
Rohtang pass needs an online permit and closes in winter.
Old Manali is the cafe and hostel area.
Kasol is the base for Kheerganga and Tosh.
Kheerganga trek takes six hours each way.
Kasol March to June is pleasant and July to August is wet.
Rishikesh rafting season is September to June.
Rishikesh rafting is closed in July and August.
Shivpuri is the Ganga camping stretch near Rishikesh.
Rann of Kutch season is November to February.
Rann Utsav tent city runs from November to February.
The full moon night is the best night for the White Rann.
Bhuj to Dhordo takes two hours by road.
Udaipur is best from October to March.
Udaipur summer temperature is above forty degrees.
Jaisalmer desert camp nights are cold in December.
Dharamshala Triund trek takes four hours up.
Meghalaya living root bridges need two thousand steps down at Nongriat.
Cherrapunji is the wettest place to visit in June and July.
Sikkim North Sikkim needs a protected area permit.
Gurudongmar lake altitude is five thousand two hundred metres.
Kerala houseboat stays are booked per night in Alleppey.
Kerala monsoon is June to August.
Andaman ferry cancellations are common from June to September.
Havelock Radhanagar beach is the sunset beach.
Scuba in Andaman needs no swimming skill for a discovery dive.
Kashmir Gulmarg gondola runs in two phases.
Kashmir tulip garden season is April.
Pangong lake needs an Inner Line Permit.
