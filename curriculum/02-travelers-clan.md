# Lesson 2: The Travelers Clan

Travelers Clan is a travel company.
Travelers Clan offers group trips for travelers.
Travelers Clan offers customised itineraries.
Travelers Clan is run by the chief.
A lead is a person who has shown interest in a trip.
A booking is a confirmed trip with a paid advance.
A junk lead is a lead with no budget or no intent to travel.
A real lead is a lead with a date, a destination and a budget.
WhatsApp is the main channel for talking to leads.
Meta ads is the main channel for finding new leads.
Cost per booking is the key metric for ads.
A follow-up is a message sent to a lead who has not replied.
Ghosting is when a lead stops replying.
The first reply to a lead should go out within ten minutes.
An itinerary is a day by day plan of a trip.
A package price has transport, stay, food and activities.
The advance is the part of the price paid to confirm a booking.
Cancellation policy is the rule for refunds when a traveler drops out.
Peak season is the time when demand and prices are highest.
Off season is the time when demand and prices are lowest.
Group size is the number of travelers on a trip.
A trip leader is the clan member who guides a group trip.
