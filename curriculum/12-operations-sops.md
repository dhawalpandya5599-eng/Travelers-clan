# Lesson 12: Operations standard procedures

Every itinerary should have a buffer day before the return flight.
Every high altitude itinerary should start with an acclimatisation day.
A drive longer than seven hours should be broken with a stop.
Every day should have one anchor activity and free time.
Hotels should be confirmed in writing fifteen days before departure.
Transport should be confirmed seven days before departure.
The trip WhatsApp group should be created seven days before departure.
The packing list should be sent seven days before departure.
A check in call should be made one day before departure.
The trip leader should carry a first aid kit and an oxygen can on high altitude trips.
The trip leader should share the day plan every evening for the next day.
The trip leader should collect feedback on the last evening.
Every departure needs an emergency contact sheet with hospitals and police numbers.
Every traveler should sign a medical declaration and an indemnity form.
Permits should be confirmed before the advance is taken.
Peak season hotels should be booked sixty days ahead.
A group of twelve or more needs two vehicles or a tempo traveller.
A tempo traveller seats twelve to seventeen people.
An Innova seats six people with luggage.
Meals should be arranged at the stay for groups larger than ten.
A monsoon itinerary should avoid landslide prone roads.
Flights should be booked at least thirty days ahead for groups.
A trip cost sheet should list stay, transport, meals, activities, permits, leader and margin.
