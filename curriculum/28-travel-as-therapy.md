# Lesson 28: Travel as therapy

Travel is a help and not a treatment.
Burnout needs rest, nature and no decisions.
Burnout trips are slow stays in Coorg, Munnar or Wayanad.
Burnout pace is one activity a day.
Grief needs quiet, water and gentle company.
Grief trips are Varanasi, Rishikesh or Varkala.
Heartbreak needs novelty and new people.
Heartbreak trips are Meghalaya, Vietnam or Georgia.
Anxiety needs predictability and a written plan.
Anxiety trips are Udaipur, Mount Abu or Ooty.
Anxiety trips should avoid altitude and risky roads.
Loneliness needs belonging and shared tasks.
Loneliness trips are group batches with a bonfire.
Low confidence needs a small summit.
Low confidence trips are Triund, Kheerganga or Kedarkantha.
Creative block needs new inputs and slowness.
Creative block trips are Varanasi, Hampi or Jaisalmer.
Family distance needs shared time without roles.
Family distance trips are a Kerala houseboat or a safari.
Couple strain needs novelty and privacy.
Couple strain trips are Bali, Udaipur or Kashmir.
Retirement needs purpose and gentle pace.
Retirement trips are pilgrim circuits and Bhutan.
A crisis needs a doctor or a helpline.
The iCall helpline number is 9152987821.
The Vandrevala helpline number is 1860 2662 345.
A phone in the bag improves a walk.
A sunrise a day is the reset for sleep.
A new person at every meal is the cure for loneliness.
A shared photo book is the memory of a family trip.
