# Lesson 25: Personalities and how to serve them

High openness customers want new places and local food.
Low openness customers want known places and fixed plans.
High conscientiousness customers want the plan in writing.
Low conscientiousness customers want it handled.
High extraversion customers want the group and the bonfire.
Low extraversion customers want quiet corners and free time.
High agreeableness customers say yes easily and need reminders.
Low agreeableness customers need proof for every claim.
High neuroticism customers need reassurance first.
Low neuroticism customers are fast deciders.
The planner personality needs a day wise plan in writing.
The caretaker personality needs safety and comfort details first.
The seeker personality needs purpose and quiet.
The strategist personality needs value logic and inclusions.
The adventurer personality needs activities and gear.
The artist personality needs photo spots and cafes.
The dreamer personality needs the story of the place.
The analyst personality needs honest comparisons.
The thrill seeker personality needs the hold now.
The entertainer personality needs people and nightlife.
The explorer personality needs variety and short messages.
The debater personality needs one clear offer after a short debate.
The organiser personality needs one point of contact and an invoice.
The host personality needs the whole group cared for.
The leader personality needs the organiser seat and a shareable plan.
The commander personality needs price, inclusions and hold in three lines.
Dominant customers need short direct options with a recommendation.
Influencer customers need warmth and social proof.
Steady customers need patience and no pressure.
Conscientious customers need precise written facts.
Gen Z customers need fast short replies and instalments.
Millennial customers need experiences and reviews.
Gen X customers need value, comfort and a phone call.
Boomer customers need safety, pace and a call back.
Anxious attachment customers need a confirmation after every step.
Avoidant attachment customers need space and no chase for three days.
Saver money style needs value and pay in parts.
Spender money style needs the premium tier.
