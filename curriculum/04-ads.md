# Lesson 4: Meta ads for the clan

Meta ads is the main channel for finding new leads.
Cost per lead is ad spend divided by leads.
Cost per booking is ad spend divided by bookings.
Cost per booking is the metric that matters most.
A cheap lead that never books is wasted spend.
Junk leads usually come from broad audiences and vague creatives.
A clear price range in the ad filters out junk leads.
A clear date in the ad filters out junk leads.
The best creative is a real video from a past trip.
Reels outperform static images for travel.
The hook is the first three seconds of a video.
An ad should be refreshed every two weeks to avoid fatigue.
Ad fatigue is when frequency rises and results fall.
Frequency above three means the audience is tired.
Lookalike audiences built from past bookers perform best.
Retargeting should target people who messaged but did not book.
A lead form ad should ask the travel date and budget.
Click to WhatsApp ads give faster conversations than lead forms.
The budget should move toward the ad set with the lowest cost per booking.
An ad set should get at least three days before judging it.
The landing message should answer the ad promise within one reply.
Peak season ads should start six weeks before the trip.
Off season ads should sell early-bird prices.
