# Lesson 21: Customer psychology by segment

Anxious customers need safety proof before any price.
Skeptical customers need reviews and real photos before a hold.
Analytical customers need the inclusions list and the day wise plan.
Impulsive customers should be offered the hold in the first reply.
Consensus seekers need a forwardable one page plan.
Customers who defer to a spouse need a message they can forward as is.
Very price sensitive customers need the all inclusive comparison.
Price sensitive customers convert with pay in parts.
Terse customers need replies under forty words.
Chatty customers need one clear next step at the end.
Families with kids need safety, food and pace answered before price.
Families with seniors need altitude, walking and doctor answers first.
Women only groups need the female rooming line first.
College groups decide by consensus and need the organiser offer.
Corporate groups need an invoice and a single point of contact.
Honeymooners want privacy and a surprise.
Solo women want verified reviews and a female captain.
NRI families want premium stays and written confirmations.
Foreign tourists want clear English and card payment.
First time travellers need reassurance and a simple explanation of the advance.
Angry customers must never be pitched.
Grieving customers need a full refund offer and no formalities.
Sarcastic customers need humour followed by facts.
Rushed customers want the hold in two lines.
Confused customers need per person versus total explained.
A customer who says will think needs a twenty four hour hold without advance.
A customer who asks for a discount needs added value first.
A customer who compares a cheaper quote needs the inclusion comparison.
A customer who had a bad experience elsewhere needs the captain story.
A customer who went quiet needs social proof on day three.
A customer who paid needs a confirmation within five minutes.
A customer who travelled needs a review request the next day.
A happy traveller refers two friends on average.
