# Lesson 11: Sales playbook

The first reply should go out within ten minutes.
The first reply should ask the travel dates, the group size and the budget per person.
The second message should send the itinerary PDF with the price.
The third message should send photos and reviews from the last departure.
Social proof should be sent before the price.
A call converts better than text for families, seniors, luxury and corporate leads.
Text converts better than a call for students and friends groups.
A voice note builds more trust than a long text.
A seat hold creates urgency without a discount.
A limited seats message should only be sent when seats are actually limited.
The follow up schedule is 24 hours, 3 days and 7 days.
The first follow up should add value, not pressure.
The second follow up should offer a new angle such as a date change or a group discount.
The third follow up should ask a closing question.
A closing question is a question that asks for the booking directly.
A lead who went quiet after the price needs a cheaper option or a payment split.
A lead who went quiet after the itinerary needs a call.
A lead who asks for a discount should first be offered added value.
Added value is a bonfire night, an airport pickup or a room upgrade.
A payment split is thirty percent now and the rest fifteen days before departure.
Every yes should get a payment link within one hour.
Every booking should get a welcome message within one hour of payment.
A lost lead should be asked why in one short message.
Past travelers should be messaged before every new departure opens.
A referral should be rewarded with five percent off the next trip.
Conversion rate is bookings divided by leads.
A good conversion rate for group trips is five to ten percent.
A hot lead should never wait more than ten minutes.
