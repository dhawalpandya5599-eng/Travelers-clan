# Lesson 15: The Indian travel market

Diwali week is the biggest domestic travel week in India.
Long weekends drive most domestic group trips.
Summer vacation is April to June for Indian schools.
Winter vacation is the last week of December.
Holi week is a popular travel week for friends groups.
Christmas to New Year is peak season for Goa.
Navratri reduces travel from Gujarat.
Exam season is March to April for Indian students.
College trips peak in October and in February.
Honeymoon season peaks in November and December.
Corporate offsites peak in January to March.
Ladakh bookings open in March for June departures.
Kerala houseboats sell out in December.
Rajasthan sells best to international and family travelers.
Goa sells best to friends groups and couples.
Manali sells best to families and first time travelers.
Spiti sells best to adventure seekers and solo travelers.
Meghalaya sells best to couples and photographers.
Bali sells best to honeymooners and friends groups.
Vietnam sells best to friends groups and couples on a budget.
Dubai sells best to families and Gulf based Indians.
Thailand sells best to bachelor groups and first time international travelers.
Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Pune and Ahmedabad send the most group travelers.
Tier two cities such as Surat, Indore and Jaipur are growing fastest.
A typical Indian group traveler is twenty two to thirty five years old.
A typical Indian family traveler is thirty five to fifty years old.
Average domestic group trip spend is fifteen to thirty thousand per person.
Average international group trip spend is sixty to one lakh twenty thousand per person.
