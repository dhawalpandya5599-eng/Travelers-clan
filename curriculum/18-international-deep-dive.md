# Lesson 18: International destinations for Indian travellers

Bali gives visa on arrival to Indian citizens.
Bali needs no visa in advance for Indian citizens.
Bali visa on arrival costs about five hundred thousand rupiah.
Bali dry season is April to October.
Bali Ubud is for rice terraces and temples.
Bali Seminyak is for beach clubs and cafes.
Bali Nusa Penida needs a fast boat day trip.
Thailand gives visa free entry to Indian citizens.
Thailand needs no visa for Indian citizens.
Thailand visa free stay is sixty days.
Thailand high season is November to March.
Phuket and Krabi are the island bases.
Thailand Full Moon party is on Koh Phangan.
Vietnam needs an e-visa in advance for Indian citizens.
Vietnam e-visa takes three to five working days.
Vietnam Ha Long Bay cruise is best from October to April.
Vietnam Da Nang and Hoi An are the central coast pair.
Dubai needs a visa in advance for Indian citizens.
Dubai visa takes three to four working days through an agent.
Dubai best season is November to March.
Dubai summer temperature is above forty five degrees.
Sri Lanka gives a free ETA to Indian citizens.
Sri Lanka south coast season is December to April.
Sri Lanka east coast season is May to September.
Nepal needs no visa for Indian citizens.
Nepal entry document for Indians is a voter ID or a passport.
Nepal trekking season is October to November and March to May.
Bhutan needs an entry permit for Indian citizens.
Bhutan charges a daily sustainable development fee of twelve hundred rupees for Indians.
Bhutan Tiger Nest hike takes five hours round trip.
Georgia gives e-visa to Indian citizens.
Georgia is a budget Europe alternative from June to September.
Maldives gives free visa on arrival to Indian citizens.
Maldives transfer cost is set by the resort.
Singapore needs an e-visa for Indian citizens.
Singapore visa takes three to five working days.
Travel insurance is mandatory for every international trip.
A passport needs six months validity beyond the return date.
TCS on overseas packages is five percent up to ten lakh rupees a year.
TCS above ten lakh rupees a year is twenty percent.
A forex card is cheaper than cash for amounts above twenty thousand rupees.
International roaming packs cost less than local SIMs for short trips.
