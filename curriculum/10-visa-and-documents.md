# Lesson 10: Visas and documents for Indian travelers

Thailand allows visa free entry for Indian passport holders for short tourist stays.
Vietnam requires an e-visa for Indian passport holders.
Indonesia gives Indian passport holders a visa on arrival.
Sri Lanka requires an electronic travel authorisation for Indian passport holders.
Nepal needs no visa for Indian citizens.
Bhutan needs a permit and a daily sustainable development fee for Indian citizens.
Dubai requires a visa for Indian passport holders.
Singapore requires a visa for Indian passport holders.
Georgia requires an e-visa for Indian passport holders.
Maldives gives Indian passport holders a visa on arrival.
A passport must be valid for six months beyond the travel date.
A passport needs two blank pages for most visas.
Visa processing for Dubai takes three to five working days.
Visa processing for Singapore takes five to seven working days.
An e-visa for Vietnam takes three working days.
Visa rules change often and should be checked before every booking.
Ladakh needs an Inner Line Permit for Nubra, Pangong and Tso Moriri.
Sikkim needs a Protected Area Permit for Nathula and North Sikkim.
A government photo ID is needed for every domestic traveler.
Children need a school ID or birth certificate for domestic travel.
Hotel check in needs the original ID of every adult.
Foreign nationals need a C form at every hotel in India.
A medical declaration is needed for high altitude trips.
Malaysia allows visa free entry for Indian passport holders for thirty days.
Malaysia needs a digital arrival card three days before the flight.
Thailand needs a digital arrival card three days before the flight.
Maldives needs an online traveller declaration before the flight.
Sri Lanka gives a free electronic travel authorisation to Indian passport holders.
Vietnam charges twenty five dollars for an e-visa.
Indonesia charges five hundred thousand rupiah for a visa on arrival.
Bali charges a tourist levy of one hundred fifty thousand rupiah.
Kazakhstan allows visa free entry for Indian passport holders for fourteen days.
Azerbaijan requires an e-visa for Indian passport holders.
Armenia requires an e-visa for Indian passport holders.
Uzbekistan requires an e-visa for Indian passport holders.
Georgia charges twenty dollars for an e-visa.
Hong Kong needs a free pre-arrival registration for Indian passport holders.
Mauritius allows visa free entry for Indian passport holders for sixty days.
Seychelles needs an online travel authorisation for Indian passport holders.
Kenya requires an electronic travel authorisation for Indian passport holders.
Cambodia gives Indian passport holders an e-visa or a visa on arrival.
Philippines allows visa free entry for Indian tourists for fourteen days.
Japan requires a visa for Indian passport holders.
Europe requires a Schengen visa for Indian passport holders.
A Schengen visa costs ninety euros for an adult.
A Schengen visa needs medical insurance of thirty thousand euros.
A Schengen visa application starts eight weeks before the trip.
Australia requires a visitor visa for Indian passport holders.
Turkey gives an e-visa only to Indians holding a valid Schengen or American visa.
Egypt gives an e-visa only to Indians holding a valid American or European visa.
Oman gives visa free entry for fourteen days to Indians holding a valid American or European visa.
Dubai gives a visa on arrival to Indians holding a valid American visa.
Dubai visa costs about seven thousand rupees for thirty days.
Bhutan charges a sustainable development fee of twelve hundred rupees per day.
Bhutan accepts a passport or a voter card from Indian citizens.
Nepal does not accept Aadhaar from adults at the airport.
Arunachal Pradesh needs an Inner Line Permit for Indian citizens.
Nagaland needs an Inner Line Permit for Indian citizens.
Mizoram needs an Inner Line Permit for Indian citizens.
Manipur needs an Inner Line Permit for Indian citizens.
Lakshadweep needs an entry permit with a police clearance certificate.
Andaman needs no permit for Indian citizens.
Char Dham needs an online yatra registration for every pilgrim.
Nathula permits are not issued on Mondays and Tuesdays.
An e-visa name must match the passport exactly.
The visa desk keeps one case per traveller per trip.
A visa case moves from documents to applied to approved.
The apply by date is the trip date minus processing days minus buffer days.
