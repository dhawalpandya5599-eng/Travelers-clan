# Lesson 22: Ground operations and vendors

A fixed departure needs a confirmed bus seven days before.
A bus contract needs the vehicle number, the driver and the backup number.
A tempo traveller seats twelve to seventeen people.
A mini bus seats twenty to twenty six people.
A full bus seats forty to forty five people.
Hill roads allow only vehicles under nine metres.
All India tourist permit vehicles can cross state borders.
Interstate taxes are paid by the operator.
The hotel rooming list is due three days before arrival.
Rooming is on sharing basis with two or three per room.
Same gender rooming is the default for strangers.
Couples can have a private room on request.
Meal plans are MAP for breakfast and dinner.
Jain food needs a two day notice to the hotel.
The captain should check every room before the group arrives.
The captain holds the vendor payments list.
The captain should share the day plan in the group every evening.
The trip WhatsApp group start is seven days before departure.
The pickup point and time are shared three days before.
Departure is usually between five and six in the morning.
A head count is needed at every stop.
The buffer day protects the return flight.
Activity vendors need insurance and a safety record.
Permits are applied for three days before entry.
Inner Line Permits are needed for Nubra, Pangong and Tso Moriri.
Rohtang permits are issued online per vehicle.
The captain needs ID copies of every traveller.
The trip kit has a first aid box, a speaker, a torch and tape.
The feedback form is sent the day after return.
Photos are shared in the group within three days.
A vendor who fails once is moved to the backup list.
Every batch has a post trip review meeting.
