# Lesson 23: Marketing and content that fills seats

Reels with faces outperform reels with landscapes.
Own footage outperforms stock footage.
Every post needs a date and a price.
The best posting time is seven to nine in the evening.
A story poll is a lead list.
Every comment needs a reply within an hour.
Every DM is a lead.
A carousel is the format for what the price includes.
A testimonial reel is a traveller talking for twenty seconds.
Google post validity is seven days.
Twenty reviews in thirty days is the local ranking target.
Every review needs a reply within twenty four hours.
A Google profile needs ten real photos.
The profile category is travel agency.
The organiser offer fills college and office trips.
Housing societies are a lead source for family batches.
HR managers are a lead source for corporate offsites.
A referral link per past traveller is the referral tracker.
Broadcast lists hold two hundred fifty six contacts.
A broadcast is sent only to opted in contacts.
A broadcast includes a stop line.
Past traveller early access is forty eight hours before the public.
A countdown story works in the last seven days.
The seat count on screen creates urgency.
Festival campaign start is five weeks before the festival.
A UTM link per channel is the source tracker for bookings.
Meta ads should run only after the profile and the number exist.
Cost per booking is the only ad metric that matters.
Retargeting should target people who messaged but did not book.
A lookalike audience is built from past travellers.
Ad creative should show the captain and the group.
The ad call to action is WhatsApp us.
The website trip cards show seats left live.
The website chat bubble handoff is WhatsApp.
