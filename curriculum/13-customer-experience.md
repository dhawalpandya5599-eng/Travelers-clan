# Lesson 13: Customer experience

A customer remembers the first reply and the last day of the trip.
Every message from a lead should be acknowledged within ten minutes during working hours.
Working hours are nine in the morning to nine at night.
A message after working hours should get an auto reply with the next morning time.
A worried lead needs the worry named before the answer.
A complaint should be answered with an apology, the fix and the timeline.
A refund request should be answered within 24 hours.
A review request should go out two days after the trip ends.
A review should be answered publicly within 24 hours.
A negative review should be answered with the fix and a private call.
Trip photos should be shared with travelers within one week.
A birthday on a trip should get a cake.
A honeymoon couple should get a room decoration on the first night.
Seniors should get ground floor rooms and a slower pace.
Solo women travelers should get the female trip leader's number before departure.
Every traveler should get the trip leader's number before departure.
A delayed flight should trigger a message from the clan within thirty minutes.
A trip change should be told to travelers before they find out themselves.
A happy traveler refers one new traveler on average.
A referral should be asked for one week after the trip.
Past travelers should get first access to new departures.
