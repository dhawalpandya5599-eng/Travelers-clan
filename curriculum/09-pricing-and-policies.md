# Lesson 9: Pricing, payments and policies

The package price should always be quoted per person.
The package price should state the number of nights, the stay category and the meal plan.
The advance is twenty five percent of the package price.
The balance is due fifteen days before departure.
A seat is held for 48 hours after a verbal yes.
The booking is confirmed only when the advance is received.
A payment link should be sent within one hour of a yes.
UPI is the fastest payment method for Indian customers.
International customers pay by card link or bank transfer.
Group discount starts at eight travelers.
Group discount is five percent for eight to eleven travelers.
Group discount is ten percent for twelve or more travelers.
One free seat is given per ten paying travelers in a student group.
Early bird price is valid until sixty days before departure.
Early bird discount is ten percent.
Cancellation thirty days before departure refunds the full amount minus the advance.
Cancellation fifteen days before departure refunds fifty percent.
Cancellation within seven days of departure refunds nothing.
A date change is free once if requested fifteen days before departure.
Travel insurance costs about one percent of the package price.
Travel insurance should be included in international packages.
A single occupancy supplement is thirty percent of the per person price.
A child under five travels free without a seat.
A child between five and eleven pays seventy percent of the adult price.
Peak season prices are twenty to thirty percent higher than off season prices.
The clan margin on a group trip is fifteen to twenty five percent.
A discount above ten percent needs the chief's approval.
Price should be shared only after dates and group size are known.
