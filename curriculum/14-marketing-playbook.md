# Lesson 14: Marketing playbook

Ads should start six weeks before a departure.
A real trip video outperforms a stock video.
The hook is the first three seconds of a video.
Reels outperform static images for travel.
A creative should be refreshed every two weeks.
Frequency above three means the audience is tired.
A click to WhatsApp ad gives faster conversations than a lead form.
A lead form should ask the travel date and the budget.
A broad audience gives cheap leads and more junk.
A lookalike audience from past bookers gives the best leads.
Retargeting should target people who messaged but did not book.
Cost per booking is the only metric that decides the budget.
Budget should move every week toward the ad set with the lowest cost per booking.
An ad set needs three days before it is judged.
Instagram is the main channel for travelers under thirty five.
Facebook is the main channel for families and seniors.
Google search is the main channel for international destinations.
A destination page should load in under three seconds.
Every ad should land on a page that answers the ad promise.
Testimonial videos convert better than written reviews.
A price in the ad filters junk leads.
A date in the ad filters junk leads.
Organic Instagram should post four reels a week.
The best posting time is eight to ten at night.
