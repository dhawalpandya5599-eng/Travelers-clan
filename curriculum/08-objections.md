# Lesson 8: Objection handling (what a lead says, what the clan answers)

The price objection is the most common objection.
The price objection should be answered with inclusions before any discount.
A discount should never be the first answer to a price objection.
The safety objection should be answered with the trip leader profile, the doctor on call and past traveler reviews.
The food objection should be answered with the exact meal plan and the veg or Jain arrangement at every stop.
The hidden costs objection should be answered with a written list of inclusions and exclusions.
The dates objection should be answered with the next two departures and a date change policy.
The parents permission objection should be answered with a call to the parents and the safety plan.
The group cannot decide objection should be answered with a seat hold of 48 hours and a group payment link.
The altitude objection should be answered with acclimatisation days, oxygen in the vehicle and a medical declaration.
The photos objection should be answered with real photos from the last departure, not stock images.
The refund objection should be answered with the cancellation policy in writing.
The crowded group objection should be answered with the batch size and a private trip option.
The wifi objection should be answered with a speed test screenshot from the stay.
The invoice objection should be answered with a GST invoice sample and vendor documents.
The too far objection should be answered with flight options and the pickup plan.
The weather objection should be answered with the season facts and the weather clause.
An unanswered objection is the second most common reason a lead is lost.
Every objection should be answered within one hour.
An objection answered with proof converts twice as often as an objection answered with words.
A lead who objects is more interested than a lead who stays silent.
The follow up after an objection should restate the answer and offer a call.
