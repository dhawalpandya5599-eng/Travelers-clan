# Lesson 26: Business techniques and strategies

The fix for no leads is the organiser offer.
The second fix for no leads is a Google Business Profile with reviews.
The third fix for no leads is one reel a day.
The fix for low conversion is replying in two minutes.
Low conversion is fixed by one price and one next step.
Low conversion is fixed by a twenty four hour hold without advance.
Cancellations are fixed by a transferable advance.
Cancellations are reduced by a pre trip WhatsApp group.
The fix for low fill is launching six weeks out.
Low fill is fixed by past traveller early access.
The fix for low margin is pricing at twenty percent margin on ground cost.
Low margin is fixed by added value instead of discounts.
Bad reviews are fixed by a founder call within twenty four hours.
Seasonality is fixed by counter season products.
Seasonality is fixed by workations in shoulder months.
Competition is beaten by the captain on every trip.
Competition is beaten by inclusion transparency.
Competition is beaten by niche batches.
Cash flow is protected by a separate account for advances.
The first growth milestone is one full bus.
Growth comes from repeat travellers.
Growth comes from partner channels like colleges and HR.
Fixed departures are more scalable than custom trips.
The first reply is the winner of the lead.
Speed beats cleverness in sales.
A concrete offer beats a request for support.
Proof beats promises for skeptical customers.
A hold beats a discount for fence sitters.
A merged batch beats a cancelled batch.
A written policy beats an argument.
A monthly report needs leads, bookings, revenue and margin.
Every lost lead needs a reason logged.
Every decision needs a number behind it.
