Indian metro young professionals are a customer cohort.
Indian metro young professionals usually come from Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune.
Indian metro young professionals are 24 to 34 years old.
Indian metro young professionals decide in a way that is fast, peer-driven, weekend-constrained.
Indian metro young professionals prefer WhatsApp text.
Indian metro young professionals have a budget of 15000 to 40000 per person.
Indian metro young professionals need long weekend dates, Instagram-worthy spots, nightlife or cafes, quick itinerary PDF.
Indian metro young professionals worry about leave approval and price per person.
Indian metro young professionals should be spoken to in a crisp and upbeat tone.
Indian metro young professionals are convinced by limited seats, friends already booked, early-bird price.
Indian metro young professionals are high in openness and extraversion.
