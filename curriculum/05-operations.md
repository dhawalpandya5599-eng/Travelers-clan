# Lesson 5: Trips and operations

An itinerary is a day by day plan of a trip.
A package price includes transport, stay, food and activities.
A package price excludes flights unless stated.
The advance is usually twenty to thirty percent of the package price.
The balance is due before departure.
Group size for a group trip is usually twelve to twenty travelers.
A trip leader is the clan member who guides a group trip.
A trip leader should share a WhatsApp group one week before departure.
A packing list should be shared one week before departure.
Travel insurance should be recommended for every trip.
A buffer day protects the plan from delays.
Peak season is when demand and prices are highest.
Off season is when demand and prices are lowest.
Ladakh season is June to September.
Spiti season is June to October.
Goa season is November to February.
Kerala season is October to March.
Manali is good in both summer and winter.
Rajasthan season is October to March.
Meghalaya season is October to May.
Monsoon is the off season for most mountain trips.
A review request should go out two days after the trip ends.
Photos from the trip should be posted within one week.
A customised itinerary is a trip planned for one private group.
A fixed departure is a group trip on a fixed date open to anyone.
