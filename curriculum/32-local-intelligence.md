# Lesson 32: Local intelligence

UPI does not work in Thailand.
UPI works at some shops in Dubai.
UPI does not work in Bali.
UPI works at many shops in Nepal.
UPI works at many shops in Bhutan.
Indian rupee notes of one hundred and below are accepted in Nepal.
Indian two thousand rupee notes are not accepted in Nepal or Bhutan.
Only postpaid SIM cards work in Ladakh.
Kaza has the only ATM in Spiti.
Gujarat is a dry state.
Bhutan has a dry day on Tuesdays.
Alcohol in Dubai is sold only in licensed hotels and bars.
Tipping is never expected in Japan.
Tipping is not expected in Singapore.
Baksheesh is expected in Egypt.
Thailand uses the Grab app for taxis.
Dubai uses the Careem app for taxis.
Georgia uses the Bolt app for taxis.
Kenya uses M-Pesa for payments.
The tourist police number in Thailand is one one five five.
The emergency number in Europe is one one two.
The emergency number in Australia is triple zero.
Dubai uses the type G plug.
Thailand uses type A and C plugs.
Japan uses the type A plug.
Thailand is one and a half hours ahead of India.
Dubai is one and a half hours behind India.
Nepal is fifteen minutes ahead of India.
Bali is two and a half hours ahead of India.
Jet ski damage claims are a common scam in Thailand.
Money changers with sleight of hand are a scam in Bali.
Pickpockets work the Eiffel tower and Paris metro doors.
Temples in Thailand require covered shoulders and knees.
Mosques in Dubai require covered hair for women.
Tap water is safe in Singapore, Japan, Georgia and Europe.
Bottled water is the rule in Thailand, Bali, Vietnam and Egypt.
Vegetarian food is hard in Japan because dashi contains fish.
Jain food is easy in Dubai and Singapore.
Jay means vegan in Thai.
Chay means vegetarian in Vietnamese.
Film a rental scooter before riding it.
The captain sends the local guide in the trip group.
