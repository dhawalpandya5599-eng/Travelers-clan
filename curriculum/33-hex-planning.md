# Lesson 33: Hex planning, hotel zones and feasibility

The planner divides a destination into hexagonal cells like Uber H3.
A hex cell at resolution one has a three kilometre edge.
The hotel zone is the cell with the lowest hours weighted travel to every attraction.
The hotel zone also pulls towards the common pickup point.
Baga and Calangute are the hotel zone for North Goa.
Downtown is the hotel zone for Dubai.
Patong is the hotel zone for Phuket.
Mall Road is the hotel zone for Manali.
Sukhumvit and Pratunam are the hotel zones for Bangkok.
A sightseeing day starts at half past eight and ends at half past seven.
An arrival day starts at one in the afternoon.
A departure day ends at three in the afternoon.
A full day excursion never goes on the arrival or departure day.
A city day allows at most two hundred ten minutes in the vehicle.
Sunset spots go in the last daylight slot.
Markets with weekly days are planned on their open day.
Out of season attractions are left out of the plan.
Attractions more than forty five kilometres from the base need a second base or an excursion day.
A hotel is ranked by a Bayesian review average towards four point one with one hundred pseudo reviews.
Google and Booking reviews count fully and own website reviews count less.
A hotel with fewer than fifty reviews on a reliable source needs a site visit.
Location counts for thirty percent of the hotel score.
Reviews count for fifty percent of the hotel score.
Stars count for twenty percent of the hotel score.
